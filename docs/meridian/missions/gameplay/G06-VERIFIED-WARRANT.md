## Secured capture implementation gap

Bind a single terminal outcome to the exact admitted actor and mission. The capture path needs an actual commander-attributed EMP electrical disable followed by authorised impound or safe, authorised empty-hull salvage that makes the actor unable to resume the threat. Laser or missile shield depletion can help. EMP also drains the impacted shield bank before electrical reserve, so a sufficiently powerful pulse can start from intact shields. More pulses use the actual remaining charge, reserve and recharge. Any occupants must be cleared through real authorised custody before destructive work. Temporary disable, target disappearance, another hull or an uncompleted tow leave the warrant unresolved.

The Voss report must validate the exact captured actor, actual disable and secured receipt. Reuse the same exclusive terminal group, report location, once-only fee and clue across both outcomes. A capture supplies no destruction kill points or bounty. A later destruction cannot replace a recorded capture or pay again. Any separate fabrication right follows the authorised salvage acquisition rules and its own proof. [Authored salvage rule](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/c70110b292077588a0855fb48127adfb1b611c9d/docs/meridian/missions/gameplay/SALVAGE-ACQUISITION.md#L86), [Current destruction-only finish gate](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/OriginalVerifiedWarrant.swift#L121)

## Failure, cold Continue and owners

Actual non-player destruction fails the job with zero payment or clue. Before a qualifying outcome, Voss's **Withdraw this warrant** closes it for zero payment. A subsequent valid offer must create a distinct native instance and actor. Continue keeps the actor, exact outcome, station report and single committed settlement. Retain rehashed attribution/report tamper rejection and retry after writer failure. The independent no-cost core inquiry can recover only the missing clue.

- **G4 physical requirement.** Verify loaded player hull, paid jumps, actual Avenor departure and return, and the real scoped SH023 actor's approach and weapons. Capture qualification also requires measured EMP and secure custody for the exact actor.
- **MC1 mission requirement, via Root and G1.** Qualify the current ordinary destruction, report and cold Continue. Bind the designed secured capture to closed physical receipts and update the Lua report selection, native finish and historical validation together. Test temporary disable, wrong actor, insecure custody, non-player destruction, duplicate settlement and cold tamper refusal for both outcomes.
