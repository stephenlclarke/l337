## Offer and story

Captain Elara Venn commissions an investigation at Avenor's living main orbital. Three near-collisions on the clinic freight lane have been blamed on Captain Orin Sorel. The buoys agree on position and disagree on time. Venn wants measured proof before clearing Sorel or reopening the lane. Sorel grounded the damaged surveyor after a freighter was guided across his bow. The booking audit reveals two paid reservations with the same certificate and a clock offset used to hide the overlap.

On Station Contracts, review and confirm Venn's offer with a writable commander save, three distinct systems recorded in native main-station history and two actual ship-type identifications. There is no rank, kill-count or previous campaign gate. The finished-game catalogue assigns the original story to **Truth for Sale** and retains a free core clue route. The exact native offer predicate checked here does not perform that product-entitlement join. ## Failure, recovery and cold Continue

The investigation has no overall countdown. Completed measurements, source identities, installed fittings, custody, physical loss, choice and settlement persist. Cold Continue must reopen the first incomplete stage on the same commander. A stale review must be reissued against the current station or live target. Review and cancellation never transfer items or pay. Verify both Venn branches on independent careers, including an intervening Continue after the disposition. The authored clue receipt is `l337.unwritten-route/clue/meridian-offset`. A repeated terminal action or reload must preserve one award and one payment.

Use only original-source-authorized replacements for clock losses or an unused registered clinic hull and consenting pilot for a failed escort. Retain each actual casualty and loss. A replacement's own real arrival is required. Destruction of the recorder or standard leaves earlier confirmed work intact and prevents a false final archive or repair. A later loss after valid completion must not reverse a valid terminal award. [Clock loss policy](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/NativeGlassMeridianSource.swift#L4), [Timing-standard loss](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/NativeC01TimingStandardPickup.swift#L495), [Clinic reserve and loss](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/NativeC01ClinicEscortBoard.swift#L17), [Terminal history](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/NativeC01CompletionBoard.swift#L274)
