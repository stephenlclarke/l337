## Offer, route and fit

The **Avenor G01-S007 mining office** grants permission for the single untouched natural **field 5, rock 0**. Extract in Avenor space, then make the one actual jump to **Renas G01-S185 main orbital** for processing and separate receiving. The jump is **3.365 LY**, using 34 fuel tenths at the stock multiplier. The fee is **780 + 110 × L + 80 × H CR**, giving **890 CR at L1/H0**. The job has no deposit, processing fee, donation, berth requirement or deadline. Reserve **12 actual hold tonnes**, and fit the targeting core, Mining targeting module, real mining work tool and Fuel Scoops. Check the actual equipment-unit budget independently from hold. The ordinary SH025 Peregrine Mk V has one intrinsic operating place, a 92 t nominal hold, 9 LY hull reach and a 13 LY tank. Check current tool fit, reach and the swept departure and loaded Renas approach. Keep the same approved carrier through processing and receiving. The source can be issued only while its original twenty-tonne yield is still pristine. No paid refinery fitting is needed. Renas performs the grading.
