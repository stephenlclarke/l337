Fly **Avenor → Renas G01-S185 → Avenor**, 3.365 LY per leg and **6.730 LY** in total. At the stock multiplier the legs use 34 fuel tenths each, or 68 total. ## Failure, cold Continue and owners

Actual destruction of an unread registered marker prompts a return to the Avenor office to cancel without payment or clue. A missing or unloaded body supplies no destruction proof. Destroying a previously measured marker preserves its committed reading. **Avenor / cancel survey permission** follows the owner after a legitimate hull exchange. A replacement carrier cannot complete the original permit. A refused cancellation writer preserves the active case for a fresh review. The finite permission remains closed after cancellation. The independent no-cost core inquiry can recover only the missing clue.
