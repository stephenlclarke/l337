## Offer, route and fit

SOREL at **Avenor G01-S007 main orbital** offers passage for **Lysa Renn and Tovan Hale** of the finite batch `avenor-orbital-outlook-01`. Their native replies cover the entire itinerary. Fly **Avenor → Renas G01-S185 → Avenor**, with three observations in Renas space and actual care reception back at Avenor. Each leg is **3.365 LY** and the round trip **6.730 LY**. At the stock multiplier each leg uses **34 fuel tenths**, or 68 for both. The quoted fee is **650 + 110 × L + 80 × H CR**, giving **870 CR at L2/H0**. The mission is untimed and requires zero deposit, donation or cargo. [Production offer](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/Resources/ScriptPacks/l337-orbital-outlook/manifest.toml#L13), [World coordinates](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/Resources/OriginalWorlds/catalogue.json), [Fuel arithmetic](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/FuelStorage.swift#L21)

Fit two separate empty, private, awake-compatible cabin modules with working life support. They use **10 t and two equipment units**. A double cabin supplies one private reservation. Fit a working targeting core for observation. The SH025 Peregrine Mk V's nominal 92 t hold, one intrinsic operating place, 9 LY hull jump limit and 13 LY tank provide a candidate baseline. Check usable capacity, cash, current fuel and actual fitted swept clearance. With a full stock tank and no other fuel use, the two route legs leave 62 fuel tenths. Ordinary qualification requires actual Avenor departure and return entry with both cabins occupied. Normal observation takes place in Renas space. [Cabin grades and private booking](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/PassengerCabinGrades.swift#L98), [Peregrine design](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/docs/fleet-design-v2/ships/SH025.toml#L39), [Peregrine art](../../assets/ships/SH025.png)

## Play the three authored stages

| Stage | Player action and qualifying evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 1. `accept` | At Avenor use **Request replies**. Obtain both current native acceptances for the whole itinerary and then separately confirm **Board both**. Board these exact two people into two distinct working cabins in one operation. a missing berth, stale reply or three active Original reservations refuses boarding. |
| 2. `perform` | Fly to Renas with both passengers. In forward view, use normal targeting to select each next admitted marker in order. Hold each qualifying view for 12 native flight seconds, then return with the same living pair to the real Avenor main orbital after the viewing visit. |
| 3. `receive` | At **Avenor return reception** review **Receive both**. Require all three saved readings, the newer actual Avenor return dock and the two original living identities. The transfer releases both cabin places, pays 870 CR once and grants the first trusted `l337.unwritten-route/clue/orbital-outlook-family-trace`. |

The Renas encounter `orbital-views` admits stationary unarmed **outlook-one**, **outlook-two** and **outlook-three** in that order, at the declared native offsets `(-5000, 0, 20000)`, `(0, 4000, 24000)` and `(5000, 0, 28000)`. The marker reuses SH040 geometry with a dedicated inert NPC definition. Select the exact next marker, aim within **2°**, and hold at **250–1,500 surface flight units** with the marker as the nearest actual forward raycast hit. The observer advances automatically in contiguous positive native intervals up to 0.1 s. Pause freezes dwell. Changing target, losing qualified aim or sight, an interrupted native interval or loading resets only unfinished dwell. A committed reading survives, and a refused writer can retry only the already measured pending reading. ## Failure, cold Continue and owners

Actual destruction of an unread registered marker preserves the earlier readings and prompts a real return of both passengers to Avenor care. Confirm **Release both** from the Cancel review there to close the job without payment or clue. A missing body supplies no destruction proof. Destruction after a marker's reading preserves that reading. At a qualifying return with all three views, use **Receive both**. Actual passenger loss closes the finite offer with no payment or clue. The independent no-cost core inquiry can recover only the missing clue. [Unread marker notice](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/NativeOrbitalOutlookMarkerLoss.swift#L3), [Receiving and cancellation gates](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/NativeOrbitalOutlookJourneyBoard.swift#L92), [Correction test cases](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Tests/L337CoreTests/NativeOrbitalOutlookMarkerLossTests.swift#L39)
