## Authored offer and proposed route

Holt verifies a specific civilian corridor threat from **two individually registered armed Larvaal attackers**. The offer is untimed with zero player cargo, berths, deposit or donation. Preserve the authored **1,250 + 150 × L + 80 × H CR** quote. The player needs a real reachable route and fitted means of achieving a lawful terminal. A useful baseline is a working front laser, with EMP and approved secure recovery gear for the optional capture branch.

**Proposed defaults for Root approval.** Use Captain Tamsin Holt's **Omdogan G04-S040 main orbital** as issuer and **Lobagom G04-S249 main orbital** as the civilian's arrival berth and final report office. The route is **1.945 LY** from current catalogue coordinates, 19 fuel tenths at the stock multiplier, **L1/H0 and 1,400 CR**. The premium is a quote input. Holt's established Omdogan contact supplies story context without imposing any Quiet Current progression prerequisite. Require the original civilian carrier's own crew, current drive and fuel, fit and docking clearance. SH025 Peregrine Mk V is a proposed physical courier baseline, with one intrinsic operating place, 92 t nominal hold, 9 LY hull reach and 13 LY tank. Use the same registered crewed carrier at all endpoints. Check the current physical and native docking admission for both NPC and player. [Holt's established contact](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/Resources/ScriptPacks/l337-quiet-choir/manifest.toml#L13), [World coordinates](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/Sources/L337Core/Resources/OriginalWorlds/catalogue.json), [Peregrine design](https://github.com/stephenlclarke/l337/blob/19a177e66093716928947f7246e0b664e4a6ad07/docs/fleet-design-v2/ships/SH025.toml#L39)

**Proposed combat roles.** Use SH115 **Larvaal Lancer** as the single forward interceptor and SH118 **Larvaal Reaver** as the raider pressing the civilian. Use their own adopted geometry, mounts and physical limits when admitted, with at least one live forward weapon and one ordinary missile each in the initial mission fit. These are proposed mission bindings and ammunition, not an assertion of current role readiness. The r3 designs give Lancer one intrinsic operating place, 18 t nominal hold and 10 LY reach and Reaver two intrinsic operating places, 26 t and 10 LY reach. Those crew estimates set the candidate manifest sizes. Bind actual original crew identities and occupancy before any secure capture. The two attackers form one finite cohort. The civilian holds clear of the native protected station corridor during the encounter and uses an actual safe departure and hyperspace jump to Lobagom after the two neutralisations. Select exact native poses with the existing swept clearance/admission checks before publishing the offer. ## Play the three authored stages

| Stage | Player action and qualifying evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 1. `accept` | At the actual Holt office confirm the published issuer, receiving station, quote and permitted operations. Register exactly two armed threat actors and one protected living civilian carrier, each unique to this offer. Back allocates nothing. |
| 2. `perform` | Protect the identified civilian. Obtain one genuine player-attributed destruction or secured capture for each of the two exact attackers. Witness the same living civilian carrier take its real route and enter the authorised receiving berth. Preserve its crew, damage and transit and arrival identity. |
| 3. `receive` | At the named actual main-orbital receiver confirm both distinct neutralisations and the exact civilian's native arrival receipt. Commit the one frozen payment and first trusted `l337.unwritten-route/clue/hostile-larvaal-defence-family-trace`. |

For capture, require actual player-attributed shield-first EMP electrical disable followed by secure authorised impound or safe empty-hull salvage. Preserve all living occupants under real native custody before any cut. Temporary disable and alien loot do not qualify. Count each actor once across mixed outcomes, with no artificial kill or bounty for capture. Neutral and consenting Larvaal remain protected. The safe-arrival receipt binds the civilian's original identity, real route and fuel, current carrier and living crew, real docking and receiver. Proximity, removal, culling, a different carrier and destruction cannot produce safe arrival. ## Failure, cold Continue and owners

Civilian loss or inability to deliver genuine living arrival prevents payment and original clue. Preserve real crew and hull loss. A later eligible job needs new actual actors and unique permits. The independent no-cost core inquiry can recover only the missing clue. Continue must preserve each actor's identity, damage, custody, flight visit, transit and terminal receipts and the frozen offer. Gallery should show the two verified outcomes separately from actual civilian arrival, and an incomplete or failed status until final authorised reception.

- **G4 physical requirement.** Bind adopted physical Lancer/Reaver fits and real crew, the proposed civilian carrier's paid transit and swept docking and the player's actual reachable route. Freeze safe native poses and mounted fit with original hull authority.
- **MC1 mission requirement, via Root and G1.** Implement G15.P01 actual civilian route/arrival first, then G15.P02 exact normal provider, threat authority, two-outcome journal, live receiving, durable save, clue pin and gallery. Test all destruction/capture mixes, temporary disable, neutral targets, duplicate and missing actors, civilian loss, wrong or stale dock, writer rollback and cold Continue.
- **Root source decisions.** Approve or amend the proposed route, office, civilian and attacker roles and initial fit before treating them as production terms. The authored two-threat, civilian-safety and quote formula are fixed.
