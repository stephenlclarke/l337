## A new commander leaves Avenor

🟡 Partial

Rowan Calder begins at **Avenor, Galaxy 1, system 007**, aboard a **new L337 SH025 Peregrine Mk V**. The fitted tank is full and the account holds **720 CR**. There is no accepted campaign, earned clue or completed job waiting to be inherited. The career begins with the ordinary ship and its actual fittings.

![SH025 Peregrine Mk V - original L337 ship reference](assets/ships/SH025.png)

*Peregrine Mk V (SH025).*

At the station, Rowan studies the status, inventory, market and chart. Seventeen commodities have their own prices, finite stock and mass units. A few kilograms of precious metal need not occupy the same hold space as tonnes of machinery. The trading preview shows what a purchase will leave in the account and in the hold. Small legal trades offer a living before specialist work or combat is affordable.

Rowan chooses a reachable destination with enough fuel and a plan for refuelling. A full tank and a drive's maximum single jump are different limits. A profit scan can suggest opportunities, but the journey still belongs to the commander. The ship launches through the actual station exit into a universe of eight authored galaxies, each with 256 ordinary systems and its own additional hidden PLUGH port.

In the cockpit, Rowan learns pitch, roll, yaw, thrust and the views. The scanner, target panel, fuel, energy, front and rear shields, heat and ammunition describe the current ship. Keyboard and trackpad controls lead to the same flight. A chosen spin can match a rotating entrance while preserving the forward heading; manual steering can take over from automatic guidance.

Arrival is a physical approach to a real moving doorway. Rowan needs clearance, a hull that fits and an unobstructed route with safe relative motion. The docking computer flies that approach too. A rescue-service berth may receive a tender without offering a main station's full market or shop. Later, the identity of the receiving station will matter as much as getting near it.

Before taking durable work, Rowan chooses the commander save destination. A later Save, Quit and Continue returns to this same career: its ship, cash, people, protected property, earned measurements and consequences. New Game's gifts do not return with every load. If a transaction cannot be saved, Rowan keeps the last committed property and progress rather than receiving half a payment or losing half a consignment.

The board and gallery show work Rowan can inspect before accepting. Each offer makes its journey, capacity, equipment, costs and any armed incident clock visible. Back closes the conversation; Confirm makes the displayed decision. The gallery follows discovered work and earned history, keeping later revelations concealed.

One introductory opportunity gives Rowan a concrete first promise: **eight original one-tonne Machinery units from Avenor to Renas main station**, carried as one eight-tonne consignment. Rowan reviews the supply and receiving terms, accepts those exact units, makes the journey and docks at the genuine **Renas main orbital**. The actual utility receiver must take all eight originals before the **once-only 730 CR** settlement and the consented-freight family's clue are earned. It is the beginning of the working career, separate from Captain Venn's full Glass Meridian investigation.

After that handoff, Rowan can Save, Quit and Continue. The returned career has the same completed delivery, the same cash and clue, and the same units already owned by the receiver. Continuing does not pay again or put the Machinery back in the hold. It leaves Rowan free to trade, practise or follow another offer from that exact point.

## Preparing the ship and keeping the same career

🟡 Partial

As the work grows, Rowan fits the ship to its promises. Pulse, beam, military and mining lasers have different roles. A missing salvage cutter cannot silently become a combat shot. Shields, energy reserve, recharge and heat impose separate limits; missiles occupy actual pylons and ECM spends live resources. Fuel Scoops and mining/refining equipment perform their own collection processes. Scanners, targeting and work guidance help Rowan find and approach the right object without supplying its evidence in advance.

For longer journeys, Rowan earns and fits the required navigation and drive upgrades. Looking at another galaxy on the chart does not move the ship there; each ordinary or intergalactic passage needs its actual travel and fuel. Later shield, energy, targeting and drive modules follow their own purchase/fitting prerequisites. A recovered item can provide its real capability without pretending Rowan bought its predecessors.

People change the preparation. Operating crew retain their reserved accommodation, and passenger places depend on real fitted cabins, compatibility, life support and existing reservations. Spare cargo volume is not a berth. An escort tender carrying its own delegate needs its own crew, fuel and fitted accommodation, rather than an invented passenger aboard Rowan's ship. A protected reference kit remains a loaned original, taking hold space until its named receiver accepts it.

For alternative ship acquisition, Rowan seeks **lawful salvage rather than destruction**. Where a target needs disabling, lasers and missiles can lower its shields before EMP. EMP also strips shields itself: a powerful upgraded unit can disable a small ship without preliminary fire, while larger craft can need repeated pulses. Power, recharge and shots stored in the bank are separate upgrade benefits, and real charge must return before an empty bank can fire again.

The targeting computer's fitted **Salvage Beam** draws an eligible disabled craft towards the close work envelope. Distance, target size, power and force limits govern the attempt. A larger or more distant target needs a stronger module. At the qualified close range, the salvage laser and Fuel Scoops perform the authorised cut and intake automatically. People, occupied sections, protected cores, capacity and ownership remain protected. Already disabled unmanned mission sources and non-EMP rescues keep their own permissions.

Docked at a showroom, Rowan studies the **new L337 catalogue**. Each craft turns in 3D through any plane. Current and selected ships rotate together in the side-by-side comparison, exposing differences in handling, speed, shields, energy, weapons, missiles, fuel/jump limits, cargo and accommodation. The trade preview shows the price, applicable 75-percent trade-in, resulting cash, equipment transfers and capacity conflicts.

Where a design requires recovery, lawful salvage and authorised reconstruction open fabrication access before a separate real hull quote and purchase. Ordinary stock craft follow their own acquisition terms. The new catalogue's station and environmental objects remain display-only.

Compatible portable equipment goes onto the new hull. Incompatible fittings and excess rounds remain owned in storage for later reuse; being stored does not make a module operational. Hull-bound parts stay with their hull. People, occupied places, mission property and cargo must remain valid before Rowan can confirm the exchange. The ship can change without erasing the commander's clues, known routes or permanent entitlements.

Travel, receiving and all later field operations stay in space. Former planet-side elements hand off at stations. Rowan has no ground-combat or on-foot episode to complete between these journeys.

The nine anthologies that follow can be purchased independently and accepted around the ordinary career. Their suggested orders make a readable path through related questions, rather than hidden rank gates or a requirement to buy another campaign. Shared contacts recognise only Rowan's actual earlier outcomes. Their final prizes require real resolved chapters and debriefs; the purchase itself grants no successful mission or fitted reward.
