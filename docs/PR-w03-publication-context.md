## Change

The ordinary W03 objective confirmation starts a fresh existing refusal recorder. A missing receiving row now includes its bounded first-refusal detail alongside the existing complete board context. Candidate facts retain at most three deterministically selected seed instances and explicit total/retained/truncated counts. Candidate facts are capped at 4 KiB per observation; the two existing first-only slots produce less than 12 KiB of ASCII. The existing outer assertion message stays within 128 KiB and reports truncation explicitly.

## Validation

Focused controls cover first-only retention, error-data exclusion, native and store rejection, the actual failing store predicate and real staged-command rollback. They run inside the unchanged four-selector W03 qualification. Existing native unit evidence remains scoped to its original source. The new Swift controls are uncompiled and unrun until Root assigns the reviewed candidate. Static checks must verify guard-expression/order equality, unchanged original assertions, unchanged rollback, bounded records, exact delta replay and unchanged execution safeguards.

## Limits

This closes missing ordinary failure context. It does not establish or repair the underlying save refusal, complete W03, qualify full CI or deliver a release. No guard, receipt, save schema or rollback policy is weakened. Any actual failure stops the attempt and is used for one causal source decision; there is no automatic retry or separate diagnostic workload.
